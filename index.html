Welcome to Uncle Lar's Local Pizzaria, where we serve up delicious pizza and pasta in a cozy, family-friendly atmosphere. Our menu features a variety of classic and specialty pizzas, as well as fresh pasta dishes and salads made with love.

Whether you're dining in or taking out, we strive to provide a warm and welcoming experience for all our guests.

History

Uncle Lar's was established by the late Larry and Norma Albright in 1983. It has always been and continues to be a family owned and operated restaurant.

In November 2017, Selaine and the late Jerry Stegmann took ownership of Uncle Lar's, keeping the same famous recipe that the Albrights passed down.
